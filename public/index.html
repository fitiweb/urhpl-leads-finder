Recommended outreach workflow

1. Discover a company. 2. Open its official website and confirm fit. 3. Enrich the domain if useful. 4. Verify the email. 5. Send a relevant, personalized message only where permitted. 6. Record the response or opt-out.

Respect source terms, Nigeria’s applicable data-protection requirements and recipient-country anti-spam laws. Use relevant business contacts, identify yourself, provide a clear opt-out, and never contact records marked “Do not contact”. No emails are sent automatically by this app.
